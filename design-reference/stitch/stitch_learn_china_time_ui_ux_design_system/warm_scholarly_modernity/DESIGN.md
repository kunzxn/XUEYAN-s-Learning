## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a warm, scholarly, and serene educational environment tailored for Vietnamese university students and adult learners studying Chinese. The atmosphere reconciles the timeless quietude of traditional East Asian literati paper, ink, cinnabar seal marks, and jade artifacts with modern, crisp digital ergonomics. 

The aesthetic avoids gaudy gamification tropes, loud high-contrast neon palettes, or cartoonish mascot gimmicks. Instead, it projects intellectual calm, disciplined progress, and quiet confidence. Visual weight is anchored by clean editorial structures, generous negative space, gentle paper-like warmth, and intentional typographic hierarchy that balances Vietnamese diacritics with Chinese logograms. Interaction feedback is tactile, smooth, and respectful of the learner's cognitive bandwidth.

## Colors

The palette draws its lineage from classical scholar studios: aged paper, cinnabar seal ink, polished jade, and imperial gold leaf.

- **Background & Canvas**:
  - `page-background`: `#FAF8F5` (Warm rice paper tone, preventing ocular fatigue during prolonged study sessions).
  - `surface-card`: `#FFFFFF` (Pure pristine ground for flashcards, reading panes, and interactive panels).
  - `surface-subtle`: `#F3EFEA` (Secondary module backgrounds and progress tracks).

  - `primary`: `#B93832` (Interactive key elements, brand moments, focal stroke review).
  - `primary-hover`: `#982D28` (Deeper cinnabar for hover and pressed states).
  - `primary-surface`: `#FCEDEA` (Subtle tinted fills for active tabs, tone highlight cards, and alert tags).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes multi-script harmony between Vietnamese diacritical expressions and Chinese simplified glyphs (Hanzi).

- **Font Pairing & Rendering**:
  - Latin & Vietnamese: `Be Vietnam Pro` offers precise diacritic mark spacing, geometric open counters, and high legibility at micro-sizes.
  - Chinese Logograms: Fallback chains render via `'Noto Sans SC', 'PingFang SC', sans-serif`, ensuring stroke balance and baseline harmony alongside Vietnamese characters.
- **Bilingual Typographic Conventions**:
  - Chinese characters in study sessions sit on a virtual square metric, intentionally decoupled from Latin vertical baseline clamping to avoid descender clashes.
  - Sino-Vietnamese readings (Âm Hán Việt) are set in uppercase medium weight (`label-md` or `label-sm`), distinguishing cultural etymological keys from everyday colloquial translations (Nghĩa tiếng Việt, set in standard sentence case `body-md`).
  - Pinyin tone diacritics maintain calibrated letter spacing to eliminate crowding over vowels with tone contours (ā, á, ǎ, à).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8px modular baseline grid, creating a stable, book-like pacing across all resolutions.

- **Grid Architecture**:
  - Desktop (≥1200px): 12-column responsive fluid grid centered inside a maximum content boundary of `1280px`. Gutters are fixed at `24px` (`1.5rem`), outer margins at `32px` (`2rem`).
  - Tablet (768px – 1199px): 8-column layout, `16px` gutters, `24px` margins. Split-pane views (e.g., character index on the left, character deep-dive on the right) fold gracefully into single columnar flows.
  - Mobile (≤767px): 4-column layout with `16px` outer gutters. Single focus study modes expand to full canvas width to maximize thumb ergonomics.

- **Component Spatial Rhythm**:
  - Micro spacings (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px) govern inline meta relationships, such as audio speaker icons next to Pinyin or HSK difficulty tags.
  - Container padding (`space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px) insulates interactive vocabulary flashcards and exercise options, reinforcing the serene, unhurried atmosphere.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids heavy drop shadows, harsh dark skeuomorphism, or aggressive blur overlays. Depth is primarily tactile and paper-layered:

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**:
  - Surface: `#FAF8F5`. No shadow. Baseline for canvas and background areas.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Surface)**:
  - Surface: `#FFFFFF`.
  - Border: `1px solid #E8E2DA`.
  - Shadow: `0 2px 8px rgba(36, 36, 36, 0.04)`.
  - Applied to vocabulary flashcards, study tiles, audio panels, and tab navigation strips.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Interactive Components)**:
  - Surface: `#FFFFFF`.
  - Border: `1px solid #D4C9BC`.
  - Shadow: `0 6px 16px rgba(36, 36, 36, 0.06), 0 1px 3px rgba(36, 36, 36, 0.03)`.
  - Applied during card hovering, active drawer panels, and dropdown menus.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Character Stroke Tracing Sheets)**:
  - Surface: `#FFFFFF`.
  - Border: `1px solid #E8E2DA`.
  - Shadow: `0 16px 32px rgba(36, 36, 36, 0.08), 0 4px 8px rgba(36, 36, 36, 0.04)`.
  - Accompanied by a serene parchment overlay: `rgba(36, 36, 36, 0.25)` with a backdrop blur of `4px`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a tailored hierarchy of corner radiuses, reflecting carved jade seals and smooth river stones:

- **Container & Card Elements**: Built with soft `16px` to `20px` radiuses (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`), framing flashcards and content blocks with human, organic warmth.
- **Action Elements**: Primary, secondary, and ghost buttons utilize an `8px` radius (`rounded-md`), communicating structural clarity and crisp button affordance.
- **Status & Indicator Elements**: HSK level badges, audio chips, streak indicators, and pill tags utilize continuous full pill radiuses (`rounded-full` / `9999px`), ensuring distinct functional contrast against content cards.
